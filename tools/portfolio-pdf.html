How I work

How I run every account, and how we can work together.

  1. Research and plan

    Market, competitor and pricing research, then a media plan with budget split and KPI targets.

  2. Measurement

    Pixel and Conversions API with event match quality checked, GTM and GA4, and platform numbers compared with store revenue.

  3. Creative testing

    Weekly tests across UGC, video and static with different hooks and angles.

  4. Optimization

    Budget moved by cost per purchase and ROAS with CBO, ABO and Advantage+ Shopping, plus landing page fixes from Clarity.

  5. Scaling

    Step-by-step increases on winners, catalog ads, lookalikes and broad audiences, with tight retargeting.

Weekly reporting with spend, revenue, ROAS, cost per purchase and store-level results.
