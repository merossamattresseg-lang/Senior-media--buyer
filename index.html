How I run an account

How I work on any new account

01 / Research

Market & audience

I look at the competitors, the prices and who is really buying, before spending anything.

02 / Tracking

Measure first

Pixel, Conversions API, GTM and GA4. If tracking is wrong, everything after it is wrong.

03 / Creative

Test angles

Video, photos and UGC tested together. I keep changing the hooks and the copy until one works.

04 / Optimize

Cut and fix

Budget goes to what brings orders at a good cost. I check Clarity and Hotjar to fix the landing page.

05 / Scale

Push winners

Then I increase the budget on what works, open new audiences and keep retargeting people who visited.
