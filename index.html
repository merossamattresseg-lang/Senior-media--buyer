How I work

Same steps on every new account.

  1. Step 1

    Research

    I look at the competitors, the prices and who is really buying, before spending anything.

  2. Step 2

    Tracking

    Pixel, Conversions API, GTM and GA4. If tracking is wrong, everything after it is wrong.

  3. Step 3

    Creatives

    Video, photos and UGC tested together. I keep changing hooks and copy until one works.

  4. Step 4

    Optimize

    Budget goes to what brings orders at a good cost. Clarity and Hotjar show what to fix on the page.

  5. Step 5

    Scale

    More budget on what works, new audiences, and retargeting for people who visited.
