How I work on a new account.

  1. Research

    Competitors, prices and who is really buying, before spending anything.

  2. Tracking

    Pixel, Conversions API, GTM and GA4. If tracking is wrong, everything after it is wrong.

  3. Creatives

    Video, photos and UGC tested side by side until one clearly wins.

  4. Optimize

    Budget goes to what brings sales at a good cost. Landing pages fixed with Clarity.

  5. Scale

    More budget on what works, new audiences, and retargeting for people who visited.
